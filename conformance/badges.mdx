---
title: "Badges"
description: "Conformance claims and how to display them."
---

Badge tiers mirror conformance classes: **Core World**, **Core Agent**, and per-profile additions (e.g. `Core World + sim + robotics`). To claim: pass the current suite, publish the suite's signed report artifact in your repo, and use "AWP-conformant" only for passed classes (AWP-CNF-005). "AWP-compatible" is unrestricted marketing language with no normative weight.
