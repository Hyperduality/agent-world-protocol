awp-sim in lockstep is the first AWP-conformant implementation: its report has no failure and nothing untested, with evidence attached for every `manual` row (AWP-CNF-005). The other entries pass every assertion the suite ran and leave some requirements untested, so they are self-assessed. All four come from one codebase, awp-python; the [roadmap](/community/roadmap) asks for three independent implementations.

To add an implementation, open a pull request against this page with its `report.json` from a suite release that targets the claimed revision.
