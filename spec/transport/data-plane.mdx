---
title: "Data plane"
description: "Binary frame envelope, backpressure, and per-channel loss tolerance."
---

Every data-plane frame is a binary envelope with a fixed 28-byte header (little-endian):

| Offset | Size | Field | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 4 | magic | ASCII `AWPF` |
| 4 | 1 | version | `1` |
| 5 | 1 | flags | bit 0: keyframe; bit 1: end-of-burst |
| 6 | 2 | channel_id | u16 assigned in `session.ready` |
| 8 | 8 | seq | u64, per-channel, monotonically increasing |
| 16 | 8 | ts_mono_ns | u64, session monotonic clock |
| 24 | 4 | payload_len | u32 |
| 28 | … | payload | encoded per the channel's declared modality |

- Frames MUST carry per-channel monotonically increasing `seq`; receivers detect loss by gaps. `[AWP-DAT-001]`
- Channels declare a **loss class** in the manifest: `reliable` (no loss permitted; use a reliable binding) or `latest-wins` (droppable; senders SHOULD drop stale frames under backpressure rather than queue). `[AWP-DAT-002]`
- Senders MUST NOT exceed the negotiated per-channel rate; receivers MAY request a rate change via `obs.subscribe`. `[AWP-DAT-003]`
- In the `inline` binding, the same envelope is base64-encoded inside `obs.frame` notifications. `[AWP-DAT-004]`
