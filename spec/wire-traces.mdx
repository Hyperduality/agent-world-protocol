Each trace is a JSON Lines file under [`examples/v0.1/traces/`](https://github.com/Hyperduality/agent-world-protocol/tree/main/examples/v0.1/traces). Every line is either a message, `{ from, step, msg }`, or a transport event that is not a message, `{ marker }`. A message marked *not delivered* was sent but lost with its connection. CI validates every message against the sender form of the canonical schema for its method and matches every response to its request. It also checks meaning: every action follows the [transition table](/spec/loop/action-lifecycle) with a permitted reason and reaches one terminal state; resubmissions are idempotent or conflicts (AWP-ACT-001); `status_seq` is gapless across statuses, events, session-state notifications, and admission results, and after `session.resume` replay starts at `last_status_seq` + 1 and repeats exactly what was first reported (AWP-CTL-008); frame `seq` is checked per channel against its loss class (AWP-DAT-001, AWP-TRN-008); a closing session refuses new work and answers `session.close` only after every action has ended (AWP-SES-011); and where a trace names t0, safe-state entry falls between `watchdog_ms` and `watchdog_ms` + 100 ms after it with no agent-originated message in between (AWP-SAF-003, AWP-SAF-012). Tokens are illustrative.
</Note>
