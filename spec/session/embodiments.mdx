---
title: "Embodiments"
description: "Binding, releasing, transferring, and observer sessions."
---

- Binding occurs at `session.open`; a non-shared embodiment already bound elsewhere yields `AWP_EMBODIMENT_UNAVAILABLE`. `[AWP-EMB-001]`
- On session close or reconnect-window expiry, the world MUST release the embodiment after it has entered its declared safe state (`safety_policy.safe_state.behavior`, e.g. `hold`, `safe_stop`, `park`). Safe-state entry itself is triggered earlier, by the watchdog (AWP-SAF-003/004), not by window expiry; release is the last step. `[AWP-EMB-002]`
- **Transfer.** Worlds MAY support handoff: the receiving session calls `session.open` with `takeover: true` and a transfer token minted by the current holder via `session.transfer`. The previous holder's executing actions end `preempted` and its pre-execution actions `cancelled`, both with reason `transferred` (AWP-LIF-008); an abort already under way ends as it would have. A transfer token is single-use and expires after `expires_in_ms`; `session.open` with a token that is unknown, used, expired, or not the current holder's fails with `AWP_EMBODIMENT_UNAVAILABLE` and changes nothing. The previous holder's session continues as an observer session: its grants are unchanged but its submissions fail with `AWP_FORBIDDEN` (AWP-EMB-004), and the world tells it with `world.event: embodiment_transferred` (`detail.embodiment`). A world without transfer answers `session.transfer` as an unknown method and `takeover` with `AWP_EMBODIMENT_UNAVAILABLE`. `[AWP-EMB-003]`
- **Observer sessions.** A session with no embodiment binds observation grants only; action submission returns `AWP_FORBIDDEN`. `[AWP-EMB-004]`
- Multiple embodiments per session are permitted when they share a `multi_bind_group` in the manifest; `session.open` then names them in `embodiments` (instead of `embodiment`) and every action submission carries `embodiment_id`. Binding embodiments from different groups in one session MUST be rejected with `AWP_EMBODIMENT_UNAVAILABLE`. `[AWP-EMB-005]`
