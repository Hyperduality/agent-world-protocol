---
title: "Approval"
description: "Human-in-the-loop gating for sensitive action types."
---

Approval sits between admission and permission in the [action lifecycle](/spec/loop/action-lifecycle): the world acknowledges the submission immediately with `state: "pending_approval"`, then waits for a decision before the action becomes `accepted`. The admission bound (AWP-LIF-002) applies to the acknowledgement, not to the decision.

- Action types marked `requires_approval: true` enter the `pending_approval` state after validation and before `accepted`. The world emits `safety.approval_requested` (approval id, action id, type, params, requester, expiry, and the current [task](/spec/session/task) when one is set) on the approval channel to its configured approver(s). `[AWP-APR-001]`
- The approver responds via `safety.approval.respond` (`approve` | `deny`). Approve → `accepted` (or `queued` if the concurrency group is busy and the submission chose `queue`). Deny → `rejected` with reason `approval_denied`. `[AWP-APR-002]`
- Approvals time out after `approval_timeout_ms` (safety policy; default 60000) → `rejected` with reason `approval_timeout`. If the submission's own `deadline_ms` expires first, the action is `rejected` with reason `deadline_exceeded` (AWP-LIF-008). `[AWP-APR-003]`
- **Standing approvals.** Approvals are per-action. A world MAY support scoped standing approvals and declares that it does with `safety_policy.standing_approvals: true`. An approver grants one by answering a request with `decision: "approve"` and `standing: { scope: { type, predicate }, expires_at_ns }`: the request's action is approved, and until `expires_at_ns`, on the requesting session's clock, that session's submissions of `scope.type` whose `params` satisfy `scope.predicate` (a JSON Schema; absent, any `params`) are admitted as if approved, with no `pending_approval` state and no `safety.approval_requested`. A grant belongs to the requesting session and ends with it. The `action.submit` result of an action admitted under a grant carries the `approval_id` of the request the grant answered, and the grant, like every approval, is recorded in the requesting session's audit log (AWP-AUD-001). A world MUST answer a `safety.approval.respond` carrying `standing` with `AWP_PARAMS_INVALID`, changing nothing, if it does not declare standing approvals, if `decision` is `deny`, if `scope.type` is not the request's type, or if `predicate` is not a valid JSON Schema. `[AWP-APR-004]`
- Who may approve is deployment configuration, out of protocol scope; the protocol only defines the messages and states. `[AWP-APR-005]`
- **Approver connections.** An approver is a control connection the deployment authorizes, for example by its bearer credential (AWP-SEC-005); it completes `initialize`, needs no session, receives `safety.approval_requested` for every session, and answers with `safety.approval.respond`. `expires_at_ns` is on the requesting session's clock. The world MUST answer `safety.approval.respond` from a connection it has not authorized with `AWP_FORBIDDEN`, and one naming an `approval_id` that is unknown or already decided — denied, approved, timed out, or ended by cancel, close, reset, or supersession — with `AWP_PARAMS_INVALID`; neither changes the action. `[AWP-APR-007]`
- Approval request content is subject to the audit [redaction rules](/spec/safety/audit-log#redaction) (AWP-AUD-006): `params` fields and the task text listed in `safety_policy.audit.redact_paths` are redacted in the log, not in the message to the approver. `[AWP-APR-006]`
