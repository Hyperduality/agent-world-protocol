---
title: "Approval"
description: "Human-in-the-loop gating for sensitive action types."
---

- Action types marked `requires_approval: true` enter a `pending_approval` sub-state after validation and before `accepted`. The world emits `safety.approval_requested` (action id, type, params, requester) on the approval channel to its configured approver(s). `[AWP-APR-001]`
- The approver responds via `safety.approval.respond` (`approve` | `deny`). Deny → `rejected` with `AWP_APPROVAL_DENIED`. `[AWP-APR-002]`
- Approvals time out after `approval_timeout_ms` (safety policy; default 60000) → `rejected` with `AWP_APPROVAL_TIMEOUT`. `[AWP-APR-003]`
- Approvals are per-action; worlds MAY support scoped standing approvals (type + parameter predicate + expiry), which MUST appear in the audit log. `[AWP-APR-004]`
- Who may approve is deployment configuration, out of protocol scope; the protocol only defines the messages and states. `[AWP-APR-005]`
