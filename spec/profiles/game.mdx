## Device input

A world that takes device-level input (keyboard, mouse, gamepad) offers it as the reserved action type `input_chunk`: a timed sequence of device states, one per slot, in the [trajectory-chunk idiom](/spec/loop/actions#trajectory-chunks-informative-idiom). Its params are defined by [`schemas/v0.1/profiles/input-chunk.schema.json`](https://github.com/Hyperduality/agent-world-protocol/blob/main/schemas/v0.1/profiles/input-chunk.schema.json). Mouse and scroll deltas are device counts, a documented exception to AWP-UNI-001.

- **Declaration.** A world that declares `input_chunk` MUST declare it with `duration: "extended"`, `"replace"` among its preemption policies, `concurrency_group: "input"`, and the normative params schema narrowed only by fixing `slot_ms` to a `const`, lowering `slots.maxItems`, restricting the key-name enum to a subset, or removing the device fields (`mouse_dx`, `mouse_dy`, `scroll_steps`, `sticks`, `triggers`) its embodiment lacks. `[AWP-GAM-001]`
- **Slots.** The world MUST execute each slot as the device state held for its duration: a listed key not held in the previous slot is pressed at the slot's start, a held key not listed is released there, and a key listed in consecutive slots stays down with no new press or auto-repeat; stick and trigger values hold for the slot, an absent field meaning neutral; mouse and scroll deltas are delivered within the slot and sum to the requested values. In streaming, execution begins at the world's first input update after admission and slot *k* starts `k × slot_ms` later on the world's monotonic clock; in lockstep, each slot spans one advance and `slot_ms` equals the sim time of one advance, rounded to the millisecond. `progress` is completed slots over total slots (AWP-LIF-003). `[AWP-GAM-002]`
- **Replacement.** A `replace` submission takes over at the running chunk's next slot boundary, which is the safe transition of AWP-PRE-003. A key held in the interrupted slot and listed in the replacement's first slot MUST stay down without a release, and the replaced action reports `aborted_at_progress` as completed slots over total slots. `[AWP-GAM-003]`
- **Release.** When an `input_chunk` leaves `executing` with no replacement taking over, including on safe-state entry and e-stop, the world MUST release every held key and button and return sticks and triggers to neutral before sending the terminal status. Releasing is not injecting input under AWP-GUI-005. `[AWP-GAM-004]`
