---
title: "Realtime A/V profile"
description: "Requirements for high-bandwidth audio/video streaming worlds."
---

Adds to Core:

- A non-inline data-plane binding: WebRTC data channels or gRPC streams — AWP-TRN-003
- `latest-wins` loss class with backpressure dropping — AWP-DAT-002
- Media channels: the world MUST offer at least one `image/*` channel at ≥ 15 Hz or one continuous `audio/pcm` channel, and each MUST declare `stale_after_ms` (AWP-SAF-009). `[AWP-AV-001]`
- Latency telemetry at ≥ 1 Hz on `awp.telemetry`, with per-channel entries for every media channel — AWP-TIM-006. `[AWP-AV-002]`
- Keyframe/delta encoding with declared `keyframe_interval` — AWP-OBS-004
