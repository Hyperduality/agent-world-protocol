---
title: "Sim profile"
description: "Requirements added for simulation and evaluation worlds."
---

Adds to Core:

- Lockstep time model with `on_tick` policy — AWP-TIM-002..004
- Seeding, snapshot/restore, deterministic replay — AWP-REP-001..003
- Sim-time timestamps (`ts_sim_ns`, `tick`) — AWP-CLK-003
- `world.reset` support returning to a named initial state

Intended for: RL/eval harnesses, game-logic testbeds, digital twins in analysis mode. The [gymnasium-bridge](/adapters/gymnasium-bridge) targets this profile.
