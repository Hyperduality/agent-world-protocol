---
title: "Time models (normative)"
description: "Lockstep and streaming semantics, tick rules, and what worlds must declare."
---

A world declares supported time models in its manifest; a session runs in exactly one. `[AWP-TIM-001]`

## Lockstep

- The world advances only in response to `world.tick` or an accepted action, per its declared `tick_policy` (`on_tick`, `on_action`, or `on_both`). `[AWP-TIM-002]`
- After each advance, the world MUST deliver exactly one frame on every subscribed per-tick channel before accepting the next advance. `[AWP-TIM-003]`
- Wall-clock time MUST NOT influence world state in lockstep mode (determinism requirement; see [reproducibility](/spec/reproducibility)). `[AWP-TIM-004]`

## Streaming

- The world advances in real time regardless of agent activity. Observation frames are pushed at negotiated rates with monotonic timestamps. `[AWP-TIM-005]`
- The world MUST publish latency metadata at least every 5 s on the `awp.telemetry` channel: observation pipeline latency and action admission latency (p50/p95, ns). Agents SHOULD compensate for staleness. `[AWP-TIM-006]`
- Worlds MUST define, in `safety_policy`, embodiment behavior when the agent stops responding (see [session lifecycle](/spec/session/lifecycle)). `[AWP-TIM-007]`

## Declaration

Worlds offering both models MUST implement identical manifests, schemas, and action lifecycles across them; only advancement and delivery semantics may differ. `[AWP-TIM-008]` This requirement is what makes sim-to-real portability real.
