| Scripted evaluator | Deterministic lockstep runs against sim-profile worlds | [Record and replay](/guides/record-and-replay) |
| Shadow-mode evaluator | Observer sessions comparing agent intent to human action | [Human in the loop](/guides/human-in-the-loop) |
