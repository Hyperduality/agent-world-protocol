- **"AWP-conformant"** — only for a class whose [suite](/conformance/test-suite) report, against the named draft revision, shows no failure and nothing untested (AWP-CNF-005); otherwise the claim is "self-assessed against 0.1-draft.N". See [badges](/conformance/badges).
- **"AWP-compatible"** — informal; no verification implied.
- Don't use the AWP name or logo to imply endorsement by the project.
