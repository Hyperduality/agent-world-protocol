---
title: "Brand"
description: "Naming rules and logo usage."
---

- **"AWP-conformant"** — only for implementations passing the current suite for the claimed classes.
- **"AWP-compatible"** — informal; no verification implied.
- Don't use the AWP name or logo to imply endorsement by the project.
- Logos (SVG, light/dark) live in `/logo` of the docs repo once finalized.
