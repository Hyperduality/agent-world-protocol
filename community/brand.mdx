title: "Brand"
description: "Naming rules and logo usage."
---

- **"AWP-conformant"** — only for implementations passing the current suite for the claimed classes. The suite is not published, so this claim cannot be made yet.
- **"AWP-compatible"** — informal; no verification implied.
- Don't use the AWP name or logo to imply endorsement by the project.
