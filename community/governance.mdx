---
title: "Governance"
description: "How AWP is stewarded and how decisions get made."
---

AWP starts under a small **maintainer group** with public decision-making: discussion happens in GitHub, spec changes after the v0.1 draft land through the [RFD process](/community/rfd-process), and each draft revision is tagged `spec-v0.1-draft.N` with an entry in the [changelog](/community/changelog). The stated goal is to move stewardship to a neutral foundation once there are three independent conformant implementations. Contributor conduct follows the Contributor Covenant (see [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](https://github.com/Hyperduality/agent-world-protocol/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)). The project requires no CLA: contributions to specification and documentation text are accepted under CC-BY 4.0, contributions to schemas and code under Apache-2.0, and contributors affirm they have the right to submit their work (see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/Hyperduality/agent-world-protocol/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)).
