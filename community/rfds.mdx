RFDs are how the specification changes. Each proposal gets its own page and moves through a lifecycle: **draft → review → stabilized → merged** (into normative spec text with requirement IDs) or **closed**. Every stabilization gets a [changelog](/community/changelog) announcement. Process details: [RFD process](/community/rfd-process).
