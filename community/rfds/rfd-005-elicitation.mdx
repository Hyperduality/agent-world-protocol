**Status: draft · Target: v0.2+ · Area: safety, control plane**

## Problem

The approval flow ([spec](/spec/safety/approval)) is a fixed-shape human-input request. Worlds and agents also need arbitrary structured input from a human (choose a bin, confirm a detected label, enter a one-time code). ACP stabilized this pattern as *elicitation*; the shape transfers.

## Proposal

`safety.elicit`: a JSON-Schema-described form sent to the configured human channel; the response validates against the schema. `safety.approval_requested` becomes the mandatory, safety-critical specialization with its fixed states and timeout semantics unchanged.

## Compatibility

Additive method behind capability flag `elicitation`; approval semantics untouched.
